Frequently Asked Questions

What is ZXing Online Decoder? ▼

It is a browser-based scanning tool that extracts encoded data from barcodes and QR codes instantly using client-side JavaScript.

Does it save my scanned photos or Wi-Fi passwords? ▼

No. Your images and decoded information never leave your device. All calculations are handled locally in your browser memory.
